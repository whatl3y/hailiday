# Fixture places and upload triggers

Everything here is fictional test data for fixture mode. Street and town names are invented, states are real (they choose the climate class), and no place shows a real home. Street photos are licensed real photographs from `content/photos` (credited in each frame as "Fixture photo: photographer / license", never as Google imagery), panned and zoomed like a Street View camera; decorated results come from the fixture compositor in fixture mode and are labeled as fixture output.

## How to use

- Type the start of an address in the landing page field (for example `14 Lan` or `Lantern`) and pick the suggestion. Every place below appears in suggestions.
- Manual entry also works: type the street line exactly as shown (`14 Lantern Hill Road` or `14 Lantern Hill Rd`) with any city, state and ZIP. A different city or ZIP still matches but is flagged as a partial match. Manual entries never auto-start, so even a clean place goes through the confirmation step.
- The render scenario applies to every render whose source is that place's street photo.
- Street photos are decorated directly. Under the default `STREET_VIEW_USE_POLICY=full` a confirmed street photo is treated like an uploaded photo: decorated (daylight and evening), kept privately for later looks, and downloadable and shareable with its attribution band, with Shop the look shown. `decorate` decorates in memory only, so results are private previews with no download, share or Shop the look. `display_only` makes live Google imagery confirmation-only; the fixture stand-in photos here stay decoratable in fixture mode under every setting. Contributor imagery (the Piñon Mesa place) follows the same setting under the default `STREET_VIEW_CONTRIBUTOR_IMAGERY=decorate`: it is never auto-started, so it is confirmed first, then decorated and credited to its own photographer; `STREET_VIEW_CONTRIBUTOR_IMAGERY=display_only` makes it confirmation-only again (the app asks for your own photo).

## Street View revalidation

Before every render of a Street View photo, and in the hourly job for every photo that is due its daily check (a ready photo that keeps a stored copy or still has variants, and a stale or declined one that keeps a stored copy or still has variants; oldest check first, in pages of 200), the app asks the street photo service whether the pinned panorama still exists. The job first brings the photo's policy up to the current `STREET_VIEW_USE_POLICY`: a policy that no longer allows distribution revokes the photo's share links and deletes their public copies, and one that no longer allows storage deletes the stored copy. When the panorama ID no longer resolves, it looks for imagery within 60 m of the property's target point (kept for 30 days after the address lookup). A stored photo older than `STREET_VIEW_FRAME_MAX_AGE_DAYS` (default 30), or one whose stored copy is missing or damaged, is fetched again before it is decorated: other bytes replace the stored copy (unless a preservation hold covers it) and the render goes on with the fetched photo, while a fetched photo that is not a readable image replaces nothing and fails the render (`source_unavailable`); a photo is never marked stale for its bytes. The fixture service answers only for the fixture stand-in photos of the places above (it never judges a real Google frame, and the live service never judges a stand-in), so these outcomes can be reproduced in tests or by editing a source's `viewpoint.panoId` in a local database:

| Pinned panorama ID                                                                  | Answer    | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A panorama ID of a place above                                                      | unchanged | The render goes ahead; the hourly check marks a stored photo checked, and a stale photo whose panorama still resolves becomes ready again and keeps its stored copy.                                                                                                                                                          |
| Starts with `fxSvMetadataError`                                                     | error     | The lookup says nothing about the imagery: the render fails (`source_unavailable`) and nothing changes, so the next request checks again; the hourly check leaves the photo as it is, still due.                                                                                                                              |
| Unknown, while the property keeps its coordinates at a place above with imagery     | replaced  | Newer imagery under another panorama ID: the photo is marked stale (never rendered again) and the hourly check deletes a stored copy, but every variant and share link made from it stays. The Old Quarry Road deep-setback place reads as missing instead: its photo only answers a 100 m search, wider than the 60 m probe. |
| Unknown, with no coordinates kept (expired or never stored) or no imagery near them | missing   | Treated as removed or blurred upstream: the photo is marked stale and every variant made from it is purged with its share links and public copies, and a stored copy is deleted unless a preservation hold covers it. Without coordinates a newer panorama cannot be told from a removal.                                     |

## Upload file-name triggers

Rename a photo before uploading to force an outcome. Matching ignores case, and hyphens, underscores, spaces and dots count as the same separator.

| File name contains | Result                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `decline`          | Declined with guidance (`not_a_living_space`)                                                                               |
| `people`           | Accepted with people detected; you choose crop, keep or another photo (`PEOPLE_DETECTED` until you do)                      |
| `hold`             | Held for review (stays in screening)                                                                                        |
| `repair`           | Render: attempt 1 fails a quality check, attempt 2 passes (a relit look: attempt 2 repairs the base, attempt 3 relights it) |
| `relight-reject`   | Render: the decorations pass, every relight fails its checks (an evening look ends with its daylight look delivered)        |
| `reject`           | Render: both attempts fail quality checks (rejected, allowance not used)                                                    |
| `provider-error`   | Render: image editor fails (`failed`, `provider_error`)                                                                     |
| `slow`             | Render: the render stage takes about 20 seconds                                                                             |
| `no-anchors`       | Render: nothing to decorate (`failed`, `source_rejected` / `no_usable_anchors`)                                             |
| `moderation`       | Render: image editor refuses the photo (`failed`, `moderation_blocked`)                                                     |
| anything else      | Accepted; render passes                                                                                                     |

Scene type comes from words in the file name: `dining`, `living`, `kitchen`, `bedroom`, `entryway` or `foyer` (interior); `balcony`, `patio` or `terrace`; `apartment`, `hallway` or `door` (apartment entry); `house`, `porch`, `front` or `townhouse` (house exterior). With none of these the app asks you to pick the scene type. Example: `thanksgiving-dining-slow.jpg` is a dining room that renders slowly. The full rules are in `upload-triggers.json`.
