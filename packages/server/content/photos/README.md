# Real photograph library

Every image in this folder is a real photograph. No illustration, 3D render or AI-generated imagery is stored here. Retrieved 2026-11-09. Machine-readable data (dimensions, focal points, anchors) is in `manifest.json`.

## Rules used

- Licenses: Unsplash License (https://unsplash.com/license), Pexels License (https://www.pexels.com/license/), or CC0 / public domain from Wikimedia Commons. All photos here are Unsplash or Pexels (no Unsplash+ and no Wikimedia files); each Unsplash download was confirmed to come from the free image CDN, not Unsplash+. Each photo was downloaded individually from its single photo page or its image CDN link; no bulk scraping.
- Content: no identifiable people or children, no readable house numbers, street signs or license plates (the few visible were blurred in the master), no brand logos or text, no gore, typical United States homes.
- Processing: masters are JPEG quality 90, sRGB, all metadata stripped. Heroes 3200 px on the long edge, houses 2000 px wide (two houses are cropped from larger photos), samples 2400 px on the long edge.
- The Unsplash and Pexels licenses do not require attribution, but we credit the photographer here and in the manifest. Neither license allows selling the photos unmodified or compiling them into a competing stock service; this library is used only as product content.

## Decoration cutouts

`decor/` holds real photographs of single decorations cut out of their studio backgrounds (WebP with alpha, at most 1100 px on the long edge) for the fixture compositor. Each manifest entry records the photographer, source page and license, the object's real-world size along one axis, where it rests or hangs (`attach`, `attachPoint`) and whether it gives off light (`emitsLight`, `lightPoint`). The originals were matted locally; nothing was generated or drawn.

## Measured house anchors

Besides the anchor boxes, house photos carry measured placement lines and spots in normalized photo coordinates: `lightLines` (the lower edges of eaves, gutters and porch fascia a string of lights clips to), `railLines` (porch rail tops, one per run between posts), `standSpots` (points on hard ground, step ends, wall caps, the porch floor or the lawn beside the walk where standing decorations rest) and, for each spot in the same order, `standSurfaces` (`from` and `to`, the left and right ends of the surface the spot lies on, and `open` when nothing stands between it and the camera, such as a lawn). Houses without measured standing spots (house-01, house-08, house-10, house-11, house-12, house-13) get lights, wreaths and garlands but no standing items: house-08's stoop is a stair and a cluttered porch, house-10 and house-11 already carry their own decorations at every entry spot, and house-01, house-12 and house-13 are never decorated by a fixture place.
